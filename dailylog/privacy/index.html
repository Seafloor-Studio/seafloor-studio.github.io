DailyLog privacy

Last updated 17 October 2026

DailyLog has no accounts, ads, analytics or crash reporting. It doesn't ask Android for internet access, so it can't send anything anywhere: not to its makers, not to anyone.

Your log

Your log is the text file you create or pick. DailyLog reads and writes that one file and nothing else. It stays where you put it: uninstalling the app leaves it there. Whether it's backed up or synced is up to whatever you've set up for its folder.

What the app keeps

DailyLog keeps your shortcuts and a few settings (which file is your log, and where your cursor was) on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

If backup is turned on for your phone, Android may include these shortcuts and settings in its own backup, kept in your Google account, so a new phone can restore them. DailyLog's makers never see that backup.

Exporting shortcuts

When you export your shortcuts, they're saved to a file you choose. DailyLog doesn't send them anywhere itself.
